Grade 7: Design Movements Evidence

DESIGN CLASS G7TH / 2026-2027

Unit Title: Design Movements: Designing a Statement Watch Strap

Key Concepts: Form, Function | Related Concepts: Aesthetics, Innovation

Global Context: Orientation in Space and Time (Explorations: Civilizations, Heritage, Migration)

Statement of Inquiry: Designers reinterpret the forms of historical movements to create functional objects that connect wearers to different times, places, and cultural moments.

Session 1: Oct 4, 2026 - Introduction to Bauhaus

Classroom Activity

Students were introduced to "Design Movements". In groups, they categorized mixed images and presented their findings. We then watched a video on the Bauhaus movement and discussed its key features.

Assignment

Create a handmade A3 collage poster about the Bauhaus movement based on the provided structure.

IB Connection, ATL & Command Terms

ATL Focus: Self-Management (Organization) & Critical Thinking.

Evidence: Students practiced organization by categorizing mixed images into logical groups and planning the layout for their upcoming handmade A3 collage.

Command Terms:

Identify Classify

Session 2: Oct 11, 2026 - Bauhaus Collages & Memphis

Classroom Activity

Students presented their Bauhaus collages. We held a Gallery Walk where students used star stickers to peer-assess each other's work. We then watched a video on the Memphis style and compared it with Bauhaus.

Assignment

Choose a simple everyday object from home. Design it in two styles: Memphis and Bauhaus. Annotate the poster with features for each style.

IB Connection, ATL & Command Terms

ATL Focus: Self-Management (Affective Skills - Resilience) & Collaboration.

Evidence: During the Gallery Walk, students practiced receiving constructive peer feedback (via star stickers) and managing their emotions/resilience when their work was critiqued.

Command Terms:

Compare Annotate

Session 3: Oct 18, 2026 - Art Deco & Bionic

Classroom Activity

Students presented their object designs. We watched videos and had a discussion on the Art Deco and Bionic design movements.

Assignment

Prepare for the upcoming interactive quiz and oral exam on all four styles (Bauhaus, Memphis, Art Deco, Bionic).

IB Connection, ATL & Command Terms

ATL Focus: Self-Management (Organization & Time Management).

Evidence: Students managed their time effectively to complete their dual-style object designs and independently organized their study materials to prepare for the upcoming oral exam.

Command Terms:

Describe Analyse

Session 4: Oct 25, 2026 - Art Deco Deep Dive

Classroom Activity

We watched a detailed video on Art Deco. Students presented their designs. We had another Gallery Walk with star stickers for peer assessment.

Assignment

No formal assignment. Preparation for next week's oral exam.

IB Connection, ATL & Command Terms

ATL Focus: Self-Management (Reflection) & Communication.

Evidence: Students demonstrated self-reflection by critically reviewing their own work against their peers' designs during the second Gallery Walk, identifying areas for their own improvement.

Command Terms:

Present Evaluate

Session 5: Nov 1, 2026 - Oral Exam & Summative Prep

Classroom Activity

Students took an interactive oral quiz. Meanwhile, they sketched designs for 4 Swatch watch straps based on Bauhaus, Memphis, Art Deco, and Bionic. This is the foundation for their Summative project.

Assignment

This sketching activity is the foundation for their final Summative project (customizing watch straps).

IB Connection, ATL & Command Terms

ATL Focus: Self-Management (Managing State of Mind).

Evidence: During the interactive oral exam, students demonstrated the ability to manage test anxiety and maintain focus while simultaneously sketching 4 complex watch strap designs under time pressure.

Command Terms:

Apply Design

Session 6: Nov 8, 2026 - Speculative Design

Classroom Activity

We watched the animated short film "Mr. Hublot". We discussed the futuristic world and the concept of Speculative Design (asking "What if...?").

Assignment

Students chose an everyday object and redesigned it for a future world like Mr. Hublot's. They presented their ideas.

IB Connection, ATL & Command Terms

ATL Focus: Self-Management (Reflection) & Creative Thinking.

Evidence: Students engaged in reflective thinking about future societies and adapted their ideas into presentations. They managed their creative process to design an everyday object for a futuristic context.

Command Terms:

Imagine Create
